📖 Complete Game Rules

• Matching: Match the top card's color or number value.

• Action Cards: Ø Skips the next turn, ⇄ Reverses direction.

• Draw Powers: +2 / +4 forces the next player to draw cards and skip their turn.

• Wild Card (W): Allows you to pick any new matching color.

⚠️ The UNO Penalty Rule:

When you play your second-to-last card and have only 1 card left, you must immediately click "Say UNO!". If you forget, any opponent can click "Forgot to say UNO!" to catch you, forcing you to pick up a 2-card penalty from the draw pile!
